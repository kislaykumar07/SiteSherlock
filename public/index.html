    THE CASE FILE

    What Sherlock investigates

    01

    Broken & unsafe links

    Every internal link is followed; external links are spot-checked for 404s, unreachable targets and plain-http destinations.

    3 CHECKS
    02

    SEO & metadata

    Titles and descriptions (missing, duplicated, over-long), canonicals, Open Graph tags and structured data.

    9 CHECKS
    03

    Launch readiness

    The files search engines look for first: robots.txt present and not blocking the site, an XML sitemap, and honest 404 behaviour with a real error page.

    5 CHECKS
    04

    Accessibility

    Missing alt text, unlabelled form fields, links with no readable text, and a missing page language.

    4 CHECKS
    05

    Content structure

    One clear H1 per page, heading levels in order, and enough real content on a page to judge it by.

    4 CHECKS
    06

    Performance & images

    Server response time and page weight, plus image delivery: modern formats, lazy loading and reserved dimensions.

    5 CHECKS
    07

    Usability & safety signals

    Mobile viewport, HTTPS everywhere, and common protective response headers — hardening signals, not a penetration test.
